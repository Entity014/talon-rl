## Purpose

Contracts for preference-authority isolation and semantic authority preservation.

## Scope

Covers actor authority, critic compatibility, headroom/capacity, support coverage, simplex-edge retention, saturation, temporal decomposition, projected descent, and robustness of preference response.

## Research progression

1. establish authority feasibility
2. separate actor authority from critic limitations
3. test support and capacity
4. audit local and temporal semantic alignment
5. stress boundaries such as saturation, simplex edges, and adversarial regions
