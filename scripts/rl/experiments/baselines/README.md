Baseline and bridge experiment families.

- `scalar_substrate/`: scalar locomotion substrate and stability gates.
- `stability_robustness/`: near-fall, recovery, reward-scaling, and preservation studies.
- `reference_policy/`: reference-policy mapping, controlled training, and replay.
- `multiobjective_bridge/`: reward vectorization, preference PPO, and AMOR bridge work.
- `reward_revision/`: revised-reward replay/retrain workflow.
- `semantic_checks/`: lightweight semantic smoke checks.
